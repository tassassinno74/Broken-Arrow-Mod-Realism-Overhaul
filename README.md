Broken Arrow gives you real vehicles, real weapons and real doctrine, and then lets a 152 mm howitzer drop a shell on the tank parked next to it, lets a machine gun swat an aircraft three kilometres up, and caps every missile at the 9 km edge of its old maps.

This mod rewrites **more than 12,000 values** with real-world data and rebuilds the game around them: real armour, engines, rates of fire, loadouts and published ranges up to 100 km, every price set on real combat value, and an **enemy commander** who only knows what his own units see. You fight as a **nation**, one of twelve countries in two camps, NATO and CSTO, with the equipment it really fields, and you **choose your realism**: Vanilla, Realistic or Semi-realistic.

You play the campaign, the official scenarios, the skirmish and the mod's own modes against the AI (**Logistics Front**, **Breakthrough**, **Command Post Hunt**) alone, or together in the mod's own lobbies: co-op against the AI, or PvP between mod players. The official servers are never used.

**6.0.2** makes **China playable** (19 cards, with new infantry), makes tanks really fire on buildings, gives Breakthrough a real 3-to-1 attack and the Logistics Front AI commander a player's economy. [What's new in 6.0.2.](#new-602) **6.0.1** is built for the final game 1.2.1: it fixes saved decks, abandoned vehicles, buildings hit by cruise missiles and the launch with anti-cheat on, and adds an Automatic / Manual laser choice. [What's new in 6.0.1.](#new-601) It builds on **6.0**, made for the game's 1.2.1 update (Unity 6). 6.0 adds a Vanilla realism mode and a Dynamic unit cap, keeps vehicle headlights off in battle, makes helicopters pay for flying low, gives the AI a sharper picture of the battlefield, makes artillery leave battery before it moves, and opens the door to sub-mods with the BARO Workshop. ## 📦 Download and install

**Requirements:** Broken Arrow 1.2.1 (a legit copy), Windows, 16 GB of memory recommended (30 GB for the Highway of Death at 10 x 150 km). The DLCs are optional. MelonLoader 0.7.3 is included in the download ([github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader](https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases), guide at [melonwiki.xyz](https://melonwiki.xyz/)). A **LITE** version also exists on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/brokenarrow/mods/7): the mod alone, for players who already have MelonLoader 0.7.3.

### Install

1. Click the green **DOWNLOAD** button at the top of this page. It opens the file on Google Drive: click **Download**, then extract the archive (Windows 11, 7-Zip or WinRAR). It holds the folder **BROKEN ARROW REALISM OVERHAUL**: the mod, MelonLoader, the music, the vehicle files, the sub-mod guides and the licence (`LICENSE.txt`).
2. In Steam, right-click **Broken Arrow**, then **Manage**, then **Browse local files**.
3. Open the folder **BROKEN ARROW REALISM OVERHAUL** and copy `version.dll` and the `MelonLoader`, `Mods`, `UserData`, `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` and `RealismOverhaul_SousMods` folders into the game folder, next to `BrokenArrow.exe`. `UserData` holds the mod's music, `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` the look of the mod's vehicles and `RealismOverhaul_SousMods` the guides of the BARO Workshop (the sub-mods). `LICENSE.txt` does not need to be copied.
4. Start the game from Steam with **Anti-Cheat Disabled**. The first launch takes a little longer while MelonLoader gets ready, and a console window opens: that is normal.
5. The **mod's home screen** appears in the main menu, and a **Mod** tab in the Options window. In the Arsenal, create a deck (NATO or CSTO, then your country), or copy the default deck of your country.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Always choose Anti-Cheat Disabled.** If EasyAntiCheat is running, the mod shows a message and closes the game before anything loads: launch it again with the right option. The mod keeps the game off the official servers on purpose; to play official multiplayer again, remove the mod and MelonLoader first.

> [!WARNING]
> **The MelonLoader installer refuses Broken Arrow**: the game folder contains Easy Anti-Cheat, so the installer reports anti-modding measures and stops. Take the **ALL-IN-ONE** (the green **DOWNLOAD** button), which already contains MelonLoader 0.7.3, or, for the **LITE** version, install **MelonLoader 0.7.3 by hand**: download `MelonLoader.x64.zip` from its [release page](https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases) and extract everything it contains into the game folder, next to `BrokenArrow.exe`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not change the mod file.** Games between players need everyone to have exactly the same `BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll`: install it exactly as it comes from the download.

<a id="update"></a>

### Update from an older version
Download the new archive with the **DOWNLOAD** button. **Before you copy anything, delete the old `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` folder from the game folder**: the old shapes of the 5.9.x versions are no longer used. Then copy the new files and folders over the old ones, open your decks and check them. To play together, you and your friends need exactly the same version, 6.0 at least: otherwise the lobby list shows **Wrong version**.

### Uninstall
Delete `Mods\BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll`, the `UserData\RealismOverhaul...` folders, the `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` folder and the `RealismOverhaul_SousMods` folder. To remove MelonLoader too, delete `version.dll` and the `MelonLoader` folder. The game's own divisions and menu come back, exactly as before.

> [!TIP]
> Before uninstalling, keep a copy of your `Decks` folder (`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\SteelBalalaikaStudio\BrokenArrow\Decks`). Decks built on the mod's divisions only make sense with the mod.

> [!NOTE]
> **About the virus warning: it is a false positive.** MelonLoader places a native file, `version.dll`, next to the game under the name of a Windows system library, so the game loads it instead of the real one. That is how every MelonLoader mod for every Unity game works, and it is also the technique antivirus engines are built to detect: the file is flagged for what it resembles, not for what it does. The mod itself, `BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll`, is one managed .NET file, with no native code, no packer and no obfuscation; it only uses the network to play together (Steam lobbies and, for lobbies open to VK Play, the public BitTorrent network and direct connections).

Not compatible with other mods that rewrite unit statistics.

## 🌐 Playing together

The mod keeps the game off the official servers on purpose and gives it **lobbies of its own**: Steam lobbies, and, if you wish, lobbies open to VK Play players too. The host's PC acts as the server. **No official Broken Arrow server is ever contacted.**

### Find a lobby
- **Play > Find a lobby**: every mod lobby in one list, Steam and VK Play, each labelled with its platform: **title**, **host**, **mode**, **map and size**, **players**, **mod version** and **game version**, the host's settings, a **ping**, filters, and the number of **mod players online**.
- **Public lobbies are open to every mod player**: no need to invite anyone. Only players with the mod see them.
- **Join** only works with the **same mod version** and the **same game version**: any other lobby shows **Wrong version**.
- **Steam friends playing the mod** have a section of their own, with **Join** (or **Rejoin the battle**).

### Create a lobby
- From **Play > Create a game**, then **Play multiplayer**: a map of the modes vs AI, an official PvE scenario, a multiplayer map or a Steam Workshop map (your friends download it automatically).
- A **title**, and who can join: **Public**, **Friends only** or **Private** (Steam invitation only), with an optional **password**.
- A public lobby is open to Steam and VK Play players; tick **Steam only** (remembered on your PC) to keep yours on Steam. Friends only, Private and Workshop-map lobbies are always Steam only.
- **The mode**: co-op against the AI, every player on the same side, or **Players vs players** on a multiplayer map, up to 3 players per side, with no AI.
- **Max units per side** (90, 100, 120, 150 or **Dynamic**: no limit for the players, the AI stops at 150 per side), chosen before the game and locked for the whole battle.

### In the lobby
- **Each player picks his country in his side and a deck of that country**: on the NATO side, one can take Germany, another the United States, another Ukraine.
- Both sides, Alpha and Bravo, with each player's name, deck and country, **Ready** box and ping; in PvP, **Join this side**.
- The **recommended memory** for the map shows next to each player's (30 GB for the Highway of Death, 16 GB for the other maps). It is only a warning: a PC that is short steps down to a smaller map.
- **Launch (n/m ready)** for the host, then **Starting in 3… 2… 1…** on every screen. A lobby chat, **Invite**, **Kick** (host only), **Close** or **Leave**.

### In battle
- A black **Synchronization** screen: the battle starts when everyone is ready, with the estimated wait.
- **F8** shows the players, and the host can kick one.
- **Connection lost**: after 15 seconds without news, the host's PC takes over the missing player's units **in pure defence**. When he comes back, the host sends him what he missed and gives his units back.

<a id="rejoin"></a>

### Rejoin a battle after a crash
Start the game again and open **Find a lobby**: your host's game shows **Rejoin the battle**, for you only (a Steam invitation works too, and the password is not asked again). Your PC loads the same battle, the host sends the state of the battle (buildings, every living unit with its health, ammunition and fuel, and your money), then **your units are given back to you**. A few things do not come back: see [Known issues](#known-issues).

<a id="privacy"></a>

### Privacy
- Other mod players can see your **mod version** and your **activity** (menu, lobby, game).
- **Steam only** lobbies go through Steam's network, as before. The account that lends you the game (Steam Family Sharing) is not published in the lobby data.
- **Privacy:** lobbies open to Steam and VK Play are found through the public BitTorrent network and joined directly: your IP address is technically known to the other players (never shown, never sent to the creator); "Steam only" avoids this.

> [!NOTE]
> **Everyone in the game needs exactly the same mod version**, and the same DLCs to see the same units. **The host's rules and settings apply to everyone** for the battle.

## 🏠 The mod's menu

The mod gives the game a menu of its own, clean and in your language. A green intro, typed like a terminal, plays at launch while the mod gets ready.

### Home
- The **mod's home page**: links to the **Discord**, the author's **Steam** profile, **GitHub**, **YouTube** and the mod's **Russian page on PlayGround.ru** (kept by Radeon), the **new features of this version**, the **mod and game versions**, and the number of **mod players online**.
- Hidden: the official lobby list, the online counter, the ranked profile, the store and DLC carousels. They are made invisible, never deleted. The menu is lighter (about 70 frames per second), and purchases and DLCs are still checked by Steam.

### Play
| Card | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Campaign** | The game's campaign, untouched, played with the mod's rules. |
| **Create a game** | On the left: **Multiplayer maps** (the skirmish is here), **Game modes vs AI** (Logistics Front, Breakthrough, Command post hunt), **Official scenarios** and **Steam Workshop**. <a id="ground-war"></a>

## 🪖 Ground war

### Weapons like real life
- **Every weapon** has its real rate of fire, magazine, reload and bursts, real calibre, penetration and dispersion; one projectile on screen is one real round. Weapons are very accurate at point-blank range, and anti-aircraft guns have their real spread.
- The unit card shows a **Ranges (direct fire)** line: practical range, maximum range, and how the weapon aims (iron sights, scope, thermal). Gunners aiming by eye are limited by what the eye can do.
- **Damage depends on calibre** against real armour: a round that does not penetrate in real life does no damage, and the cards show the real millimetres. An APFSDS round loses about 4 % of its penetration per km.
- Shaped-charge anti-tank missiles do not fire at infantry, and anti-tank teams keep their missiles for armour.

### Damaged parts
When a hit goes through a ground vehicle, it can strike a part, chosen by the face hit: **tracks** (immobilised), **wheels or engine** (slowed, then immobilised), **engine fire**, **turret** (slower aim), **gun** (inaccurate), **optics** (sees less far), **crew** (slower), or the **ammunition**, which can cook off: T-72, T-80 and T-90 60 %, Leopard 2 20 %, Abrams 10 %, T-14 0 %. A hit that does not go through can still damage the optics, the sights, the turret or the tracks, or stun the crew. An immobilised vehicle only turns its turret. Supply trucks, repair trucks and engineers repair.

### Suppression
Stress rises with hits **and near misses**, up to **shaken** and then **panicked**: shaken infantry shoots wider, panicked infantry much wider, and still crawls forward. Vehicles are less affected. Calm returns about 40 seconds after the fire stops, and suppression is the same for every player in co-op.

### Infantry and buildings
- **Real team sizes** (2 to 4 men for a Javelin, TOW, Kornet, Stinger or Igla team), smoke grenades for every combat squad, and a sprint of about 300 m.
- **Carried units die with their carrier**: after a heavy cannon, a missile or a bomb, nobody gets out; survivors of lighter fire come out wounded.
- **Buildings by the host's realism mode**: Realistic like real life (a bomb blows away a wooden house, only the part that is hit collapses), Semi-realistic 2 to 3 times sturdier. Bullets, 20 to 40 mm guns and grenades do nothing to walls.
- **City fighting**: infantry in a building is hard to drive out with small arms, and a single ordinary shell no longer wipes out a squad inside; explosives, thermobarics, artillery and tank guns still work.
- Infantry bodies stay for the duration you chose, even off-screen.
- **Standing soldiers are treated** at a depot, a supply truck or the base; snipers reach their real practical range.

<a id="entrench"></a>

### Digging in and fortifications
- **Five real levels** for infantry and vehicles that stay in place, up to 45 minutes, faster near supply, with engineers or with a dozer blade. Each level makes the unit harder to spot, to hit and to drive out.
- **Two kinds of engineers** in every country: **sappers** lay and clear mines (**Clear a mine lane**) and repair vehicles; **construction engineers** fortify buildings and carry a depot's stock inside. Engineers can fortify a whole area with visible walls.
- **Special forces and recon** move, camouflage and aim faster.

### Mines
- **Engineers** lay anti-tank mines in a line; a mine is live the moment it lands, and **any vehicle** that rolls over it is destroyed, friend or foe.
- **Remote mining**, the same number on each side: M109A6 and M109A7 with RAAM shells for the United States, ISDM Zemledeliye and Grad with 9M28K rockets for Russia, 10 km range, mines that stay for the whole game, at most 100 per side.
- **Mine clearing**: mine-clearing vehicles, sappers, and KMT ploughs with their **Mine-clearing mode**.
- Mines are shared by every player in co-op, with their explosion and crater.

### Artillery
- **Real loadouts and stowage**, guided shells (Krasnopol-M, Excalibur, Vulcano...), real **aiming times** and **minimum ranges**.
- **In battery**: after every move, a gun goes through a real emplacement time before it fires, and it leaves battery before it drives off (30 s self-propelled, 120 s towed). The AI calls for its fire (60 s) and adjusts it.
- **Two modes**, together if you wish: **Fire at spotted units** and **Counter-battery**, on a battery located by sound.
- **Remote mining** shells and rockets, and **cluster munitions** with their real effectiveness.

### Movement and fire orders
- **Right-click** = by road; **double right-click** = across country; **B** = back to base by road. Empty unarmed trucks drive home on their own.
- **Attack** (**Q** on QWERTY, **A** on AZERTY) is the game's attack-move.
- **G** is **Suppressive fire** for ground vehicles: the vehicle moves up within range, then fires a long burst (200 autocannon rounds, 5 tank shells, 50 grenades, 100 machine gun rounds), on a unit or on a point on the ground. Neither G nor Attack fires through two separate stretches of forest.
- Autocannon and heavy machine gun bursts **fell trees**, and the view opens for both sides.

### Explosions and sound
- **Real blast and fragmentation** for every explosive, and trees flattened within the real blast radius. A destroyed vehicle explodes with the explosive it still carries.
- **Sound travels as far as it really does**: a ballistic missile 15 km, a howitzer 10 km, a tank gun 5 km, a rifle 2 km, with echoes at the speed of sound.
- Real light at launches and explosions, smoke and dust when a tank fires, trails of about 30 seconds that spread out.

## ✈️ Air war

### Real self-protection, real seekers
Every aircraft and helicopter carries its **real self-protection suite** (flares, chaff, jammer, DIRCM, towed decoy, or nothing at all) and pays for it. Decoys go out on their own at the missile warning. What fools a missile depends on **its seeker**, for 265 guided munitions:

| Seeker | Chance to be fooled |
|---|---|
| Old infrared | 70 % by flares |
| Second-generation infrared | 30 to 55 % by flares |
| Imaging infrared | 15 % by flares |
| Infrared against a DIRCM | 50 to 90 % |
| Old radar | 35 % by chaff |
| Modern radar | 10 % by chaff |
| Anti-radiation, GPS, ballistic | Never |

In Semi-realistic, each countermeasure succeeds 25 % more often. Several add up, never beyond 95 %.

### Aircraft and helicopters
- **Real manoeuvrability**: every fighter turns at its real rate, the Sukhois with thrust vectoring first, and can **dodge a missile**. Your fighters with no order hunt the enemy aircraft your side sees, then come home when hit or short of ammunition.
- **Stealth against radar only**: F-22, F-35, B-2, Su-57 and others are found by radar much closer, by the age of the radar, while eyes and infrared see them like any other aircraft.
- **Real engines**: real thrust, climb and turn.
- **Real endurance** on every map and a **real turnaround time** at base (5 to 45 minutes). Helicopters can **land**, engine off.
- **Aircraft appear 1 km and helicopters 500 m outside the map, behind their own side.**
- **Low flight** hides from long-range radar missiles but hands you to every gun in reach; **high flight** is seen by all, up to the real ceiling of the air defence.

### Strike
- **Glide bombs** at their real stand-off (50 to 100 km), released with the precision-strike order; guided bombs of several calibres are all released.
- **Toss strike** for the aircraft that really do it; **Rocket salvo** button (25, 50, 75 or 100 % of the unguided rockets per pass).
- **Over 160 real loadouts** on the right pylons; loadouts the real aircraft never carried are hidden.
- **Real missile profiles**: the Kinzhal, the Zircon and the ARRW fly the way they really do; Javelin, Spike and Hellfire dive onto the roof.
- **Laser designation** on the units that really carry a designator: **Automatic** or **Manual** (Esc > Options > Mod); a laser you set yourself is never moved.
- **Kamikaze drones** (Geran-2, LUCAS): five engines, three warheads, and the **DIVE HERE** button. The jet Geran-5 can only be shot down by missiles.

### Air defence
- **Two anti-air missile hits bring down anything that flies.** A 12.7 mm machine gun reaches a helicopter in flight up to 1,000 m; cannons without radar reach helicopters at 1,500 m at most.
- **You set each launcher**: missiles, helicopters, aircraft, drones. **Anti-missile** and **Rocket/bomb intercept** switches on the systems built for it.
- **A radar that is on gets picked up** by enemy aircraft; switch it off to hide.

## 🧠 The enemy commander

A second commander plays the enemy side of every campaign mission next to the mission script, takes the place of the computer players in scenarios and skirmishes, and is the only enemy in the modes vs AI.

- **A player's eyes**: he sees exactly what the game shows his side, nothing more, and decides after a staff delay. **No hidden advantage.**

- **He reads what you field and answers it**: your helicopters bring surface-to-air missiles, your artillery brings counter-battery, your armour brings anti-tank teams and a flanking group.
- **Defence first**: a real defensive line with its air defence and a reserve, recon before every attack, ambushes at bridges, crossroads and forest edges.
- **Disciplined fire**: guns that move after every salvo, air defence that never rides in the assault column, aircraft that never fly into known air defence.
- **He learns**: a technique that fails is not used twice on the same objective, and an axis where he lost units is avoided.
- **He spends like you**: a real wallet, cards like yours, supply trucks, engineers and sappers.
- **He never breaks a mission**: he checks the script before adding anything.

**An AI ally** can fight at your side when you play alone against the AI, with the same brain as the enemy.

## 👁️ Line of sight, weather and water

- **Sight is counted forest by forest**: you see the first wood and its edge, nothing behind the second one. A thick forest blocks direct fire and operator-guided missiles.
- **Heights are measured** on the game's own height map; trees stand at 25 m, and a flattened building stops hiding the street behind it.
- **An unidentified enemy stays a generic contact** until you get close or it opens fire.
- **Infantry that stops sees further** (binoculars), recon that stops even further (thermal), troops on the run see less. The same for the AI.
- **Weather**: rain and fog shorten sight a little for everyone, and the weather can change during the game. A **wind** drawn for each battle carries the smoke.
- **Water**: vehicles that cannot swim never drive into it and take the bridges; amphibious vehicles swim at their real speed, and infantry wades the fords. A vehicle in the water is hit as on land.
- **Battle icons** stay visible when you zoom out, sized to the camera distance. The fog of war is unchanged.

- **Real speeds** for vehicles, aircraft, missiles, bullets and mortar bombs, plus 10 % for every unit to keep the pace.
- **Real acceleration and braking** from the real power and weight of each vehicle, **real slopes** (an Abrams drops from 48 to 27 km/h off-road on a 10 % slope), and real off-road speeds.
- **Real ammunition loads** on both sides, resupplied by their real weight; real helicopter capacities; real optical ranges per unit.
- **The game's own "Hold fire"** holds fire but shoots back as soon as the unit is fired upon.
- **Nothing is written into the game's own files.** Every value is changed in memory and handed back when the mod stops.
- Fifteen original music tracks and an Intro theme play in the menu; in battle, the game's own music.

<a id="not-possible"></a>

## 🚫 What is not possible, and I'd rather say it

- **Two sides only.** The game knows the American side and the Russian side, so NATO countries always fight CSTO countries.
- **Countries whose main equipment is not in the game**, such as the United Kingdom, cannot be made on their own, and France is still in preparation. A card is kept only when the model shown in the game is the real vehicle.
- **No play on the official servers.** The mod plays solo and, together, only in its own lobbies.
- **Everything coming back after a crash.** A player who rejoins gets his units, their health and his money back, but felled trees, craters and wrecks made while he was away do not show, and there is no spectator mode.
- **Bombs without wings still fall ballistically**, and a commander's aircraft in the campaign only comes where the mission itself flies one.

<a id="known-issues"></a>

## 🐞 Known issues

- The Highway of Death needs a lot of memory (30 GB recommended) and loads longer; an enlarged map takes a few more seconds to load.
- On the enlarged maps, bridges are wider than the road and town streets are enlarged with the town.
- The commander can cause a short hitch when he re-plans on the big maps.
- The Arsenal card of a Shtora vehicle still shows the game's own value; in battle the mod uses the real one.
- Fast rope: the ropes hang from the middle of every helicopter.
- Some Steam Workshop maps do not allow every game mode.
- **Damaged parts in co-op**: the icons are seen by everyone, but the on-screen notice only appears on the PC that fired.

Found something else? Click the game's bug button 🐞: the **Report a mod bug** window tells you where to report it on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/brokenarrowrealism) and which logs to send. Translation errors can be reported the same way.
