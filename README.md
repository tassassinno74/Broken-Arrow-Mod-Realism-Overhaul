Broken Arrow gives you real vehicles, real weapons and real doctrine, and then lets a 152 mm howitzer drop a shell on the tank parked next to it, lets a machine gun swat an aircraft three kilometres up, and caps every missile at the 9 km edge of its old maps.

This mod rewrites **more than 8,000 values** with real-world data, reads the game's own maps and mission scripts, prices every unit on what it can really do, gives every aircraft and every gun the loadouts its real counterpart carries, gives every weapon its published range up to 100 km, and puts a **second commander** on the other side of the map who reads what you field and answers it. Since 4.9.6 you also fight as a **nation**: eleven countries in two camps, NATO and CSTO, each with its real flag and the equipment it really uses.

- **You will escort your transports**, because a rocket into a light carrier now kills the squad inside.
- **You will fire smoke because you need it**, not because it looks good: every combat squad carries its own grenades, screens are wide, and smoke blocks sight, laser guidance and fire for both sides.
- **You will stop using artillery as a panic button**, because guns have minimum ranges and real aiming times, and nothing saves you at point-blank.
- **You will fear the ground when you fly**, because two anti-air missile hits bring down any helicopter, and flying low hides you from long-range radar missiles while handing you to every machine gun and short-range launcher within reach.
- **You will carry the right countermeasure**, because flares fool what looks for heat, chaff fools what looks for radar, and a missile that is not fooled keeps its full chance to hit.
- **You will think before you buy**, because one T-14 Armata costs more than four old T-72s and you only get two of them.
- **You will choose what hangs under your wings**, because the pylon menu offers what the real aircraft carries and hides what it never did.
- **You will guard your supply trucks**, because in Logistics Front your money is the stock sitting in your depots.
- **You will fight for the bridges**, because a river now stops every tank, truck and soldier, and only amphibious vehicles swim across.
- **You will use the terrain**, because a tank behind a forest is a tank you cannot see, and a launcher that cannot see you cannot shoot you.

### A river you cannot ford
- **The water is shown** along the whole winding river of the enlarged Meandering River, and no tree or object stands in it, bridges excepted.
- **The whole river stops every ground unit**, deep water and fords alike: tanks, trucks and infantry cross by the **four bridges**. Only amphibious vehicles swim across, through the deep water. The small islands stay, and only amphibious vehicles reach them.
- The same rule for you and for the enemy: his units take the bridges too.

### A minimap that stays north up
- In every battle of the mod, the minimap no longer turns with the camera: north stays at the top. Your own setting in the game options is left as you set it.
- In Logistics Front, the blue and red squares of both bases now sit exactly on the bases.

### Logistics Front: a commander who holds a front
- He **buys units regularly** with his own money, under the same rules as you, and now pays for the infantry riding in his vehicles.
- He holds a **front in three sectors**, with reconnaissance in front. On Meandering River each sector holds his bank of the river and **every bridge gets a garrison**: mounted infantry, anti-tank and short-range air defence.
- His reconnaissance **searches the edges and corners of the map** for your depots, and finds them only from the ground, like you.
- He **hides and scatters his depots**: never in his base, at least 1.5 km apart, at forest edges and on reverse slopes, away from the main roads, never across the river, never again near a spot where he lost one, each with its own infantry guard. His **empty trucks drive back to his base** and are reloaded there for the next convoy.
- He attacks **only when he has the advantage**: a known air defence, one of your depots, or your base, but **only once his front line stands**: from 12 minutes with 85 % of his line in place, from 15 minutes with 60 %, at 20 minutes in any case.
- **After a loss in the air, he stays on the ground**: once one of his helicopters or aircraft is shot down or hit, he sends no more until he has destroyed your air defence, and his ground units go looking for it.

### Enemy commander: the end of the helicopter spam
- A known air defence becomes a **no-go zone** for his helicopters: MANPADS 5 km, short-range systems 8 km, C-RAM 2.5 km, long-range systems 12 km.
- After two helicopter losses in five minutes he **goes by road**: convoys with just the vehicles they need (armoured carriers first, then trucks, then light vehicles), on three different lanes. A road where a convoy died is avoided for 15 minutes, a mined road for 20.
- Urgent orders are given at once, reinforcements come in threes, units scatter 400 m under fire, and his artillery only fires on an enemy seen in the last 75 seconds.

### Decoys that behave like the real thing
- The missile flies all the way. Flares or chaff go out **about 3 seconds before impact**, and a fooled missile misses **near the aircraft**: no more missile dropping dead in front of its launcher.
- The salvo matches the threat: flares against infrared, chaff against radar, both when the threat is both. The game's own automatic release is switched off.
- **Escort jamming:** an EA-6B Prowler or a Su-24MP jams the radars aiming at friendly aircraft within 25 km.
- The S-300V and the Patriot PAC-2 now count as modern radar seekers: chaff fools them one time in ten instead of one in three. Pantsir missiles ignore flares.

### Explosions

- **Aircraft are caught in the blast**, even in the air: destroyed close to a big charge, damaged further out.

## 🎯 Read this first: your deck

**One deck is one army.** In the Arsenal, create a deck, choose **NATO** or **CSTO**, then a **country**: one click gives the deck both divisions of that country. The United States is USA MODE 1 and USA MODE 2, Russia is RUSSIE MODE 1 and RUSSIE MODE 2: each pair holds the complete American or Russian arsenal of the game, and the equipment of the other countries sits in their own divisions. The game's own divisions are hidden while the mod is installed; remove the mod and they come back.

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| **7 cards per category** | The same for everyone. |
| **14,000 points per category** | In every category of the deck. |
| **Real availability** | Old, mass-produced vehicles come in numbers, modern ones in small batches: two T-14 per card against twelve old T-72s. |
| **Real prices** | Every unit priced on its real combat value; the T-14 Armata is the strongest tank in the game and priced like it. |
| **Ready-made decks** | Two decks, RUSSIA and USA, are updated with each version, after a copy is kept. Your own decks are only changed when a card leaves a division, as in 4.9.6, and always after a copy. |
| **Safe saved decks** | A card over its maximum drops back to that maximum on its own. Duplicate cards are hidden. All camouflages are unlocked. |
| **Campaign** | Only your USA or Russia deck plays in the campaign, and a message says which one. Everything the mission hands you comes on top of your cards, so the scripted parts keep working. |
| **Everywhere else** | Scenarios, skirmish and Logistics Front take the deck of any country. |

## 🧠 The enemy commander

A second commander plays the enemy side of every campaign mission next to the mission script, takes the place of the weakest computer player in offline scenarios and skirmishes, and is the only enemy in Logistics Front. You choose his level on the briefing screen: script only, regular player, or pro. In Play > Scenarios, the **ENEMY** and **ALLY** choices sit under the map.

- **No economy of his own** in the campaign. He reads what you field every thirty seconds, and the value of your living units is the value he is allowed to keep on the map. He has cards like you: each unit type only in the number of its card, a lost unit back after the same delay as yours.
- **Reconnaissance first**, then he answers what you do: your helicopters bring surface-to-air missiles, your artillery brings a long-range battery under short-range cover, your armour brings a flanking group. Infantry always arrives in vehicles and dismounts near the objective.
- **Air defence never rides in the assault column.** Long-range batteries sit on high ground at the rear with a supply truck, short-range systems hide at the forest edge and move 400 m when shelled. His defence spreads left and right on the heights, with two scouts sweeping the map.
- **No helicopter spam.** Known air defence becomes a no-go zone; after two losses in five minutes he switches to road convoys on changing lanes, avoiding roads where a convoy died or a mine went off.
- **He learns.** He climbs a ladder of eighteen techniques on six levels. A technique that fails moves him one level up and to the other flank, and he never plays the same one twice on the same objective. An attack axis that failed is not used again before two others. On the pro level he flanks, gathers at a rally point and assaults together.
- **Disciplined fire.** His artillery never fires without a contact seen in the last 75 seconds, and never three times on the same spot. An isolated unit under fire takes cover.
- **His pilots want to come home**: they strike from three to six kilometres and return to base when hit, at half health or at half their flares.
- **He never breaks a mission.** He checks the script before adding anything and never breaks an objective.

**An allied commander** can fight at your side, with the same brain as the enemy. It is off by default. In the campaign, at its top level, it drops squads behind the lines by helicopter every three minutes and covers an extraction with up to five attack helicopters.

### In Logistics Front: a front, not a rush

- **He spends his own money**, earned from his own depots under the same rules as yours, buys units regularly, and pays for the infantry riding in his vehicles.
- **Three sectors**, left, centre and right as seen from your base, each with reconnaissance in front, armour, mounted infantry, short-range air defence behind and a forward supply truck, and a mobile reserve that counter-attacks where a sector is pressed. Without a river, his line moves forward in 900 m bounds and falls back when it meets a stronger force.
- **Rivers and bridges**: on Meandering River each sector holds his bank, and every bridge gets a garrison of mounted infantry, an anti-tank team and short-range air defence.
- **Reconnaissance tours** every three to six minutes, out to the edges and corners of the map. He finds your depots only from the ground, like you.
- **Hidden, scattered depots**, as the rules reward: more stock means more income, but one big pile is one strike away from nothing. His depots sit at least 1.5 km apart, never in his base, under the trees or behind a crest, away from the main roads, and each gets an infantry guard. An empty truck never waits next to a depot: it **drives back to his base** and is reloaded there for the next convoy (only the supply is paid, like yours).
- **He attacks only with the advantage**: against a known air defence, one of your depots, or your base, **after building his front line**: from 12 minutes with 85 % of his line in place, from 15 minutes with 60 %, at 20 minutes in any case. He gathers short of the target, assaults when most of his force has arrived, and falls back after heavy losses.
- **After a loss in the air, he stays on the ground**: once one of his helicopters or aircraft is shot down or hit, he sends no more until your air defence is destroyed, and his ground units and artillery go after it. He also keeps to the air timing: helicopters from 10 minutes, aircraft from 20.
- **He defends his base**: a ground unit in his square draws his reserve and nearby units onto it.

## ✈️ Air war

### Real self-protection, real seekers
Every aircraft and helicopter carries its **real self-protection suite**: its real number of flares and their generation, chaff, jammer, DIRCM, towed decoy, or nothing at all when the real machine has nothing. Fifteen real modernisations are paid choices on the card. Flares are always automatic.

What fools a missile depends on **its seeker**, for 265 guided munitions:

| Seeker | Chance to be fooled |
|---|---|
| Old infrared | 70 % by flares |
| Second-generation infrared | 30 to 55 % by flares |
| Imaging infrared | 15 % by flares |
| Infrared against a DIRCM | 50 to 90 % |
| Old radar | 35 % by chaff |
| Modern radar (S-300PM, S-300V, Patriot PAC-2...) | 10 % by chaff |
| Wire-guided anti-tank | 65 % by smoke, 30 to 60 % by a dazzler |
| Anti-radiation, GPS, ballistic | Never |

Several countermeasures add up, never beyond 95 %. A fooled missile misses near the aircraft; one that is not fooled keeps the game's normal chance to hit. The salvo goes out about three seconds before impact and matches the threat. An EA-6B Prowler or a Su-24MP jams the radars aiming at friendly aircraft within 25 km.

### Air defence
- **Two anti-air missile hits bring down anything that flies.** Long-range radar missiles leave a helicopter flying below 25 m to short-range systems and guns.
- **Machine guns and cannons hit helicopters like real turrets**: a 12.7 mm is deadly at 300 to 500 m and wasteful beyond.
- **You set each launcher**: tick missiles, helicopters, aircraft, drones, in any combination. By default everything is ticked.
- **Interception**: Pantsir, Tor, Buk, S-300, S-350, S-400, Patriot, IRIS-T and NASAMS shoot down heavy rockets and guided bombs, never shells, mortar bombs or Grad rockets. The Pantsir also intercepts with its cannon.
- **Automatic radars**: fixed-radar systems switch their radar on by themselves five seconds after stopping. Infrared missiles fire without radar.

### Strike
- **Glide bombs at their real stand-off**, 50 to 100 km, released with the precision-strike order: JSOW, SDB II, PBK-500U, UMPK kits. Heavy air defence can shoot them down.
- **Real missile profiles**: the Kinzhal, the Zircon and the ARRW fly the way they really do.
- **Laser designation** is automatic and free on the 65 units that really carry a designator. No paid option, no manual button.
- **Radar stealth**: F-22, F-35, B-2, F-117 and Comanche are detected much closer; the Su-57 is the stealthiest aircraft of the game.
- **150 loadout choices on the right pylons**: UMPK kits, KAB-250LG, Kh-59M2, Kh-38MTE, Kh-69, R-77M on the Russian side; GBU-31, GBU-39 SDB, CBU-105, AIM-120D, AARGM-ER, SLAM-ER, twelve JASSM on the B-52H; AGM-114R, Spike NLOS, LMUR, JAGM on the helicopters. Loadouts the real aircraft never carried are hidden.
- **Rocket pods** fire half their rockets in lofted fire over an area, 3 to 6 km. Blast radii follow the real weight of every bomb.
- **An emptied transport** returns to base on its own.

### Kamikaze drones
Geran-2 for Russia and LUCAS for the USA. On the card you pick one of five engines (150 to 600 km/h, real names, priced by speed) and one of three warheads (fragmentation, small-area blast, shaped charge). It cruises high, out of reach of machine guns, and dives almost vertically onto its target. Press **DIVE HERE**, then click the ground: the drone dives and explodes there.

## 👁️ Line of sight

- **Sight is counted forest by forest.** You see the first wood and its edge, nothing behind the second one.
- **Heights are measured, not guessed**: the mod reads the game's own height map, three metres per pixel. Trees stand at 25 m, roofs are the real roof of each building, and a flattened building stops hiding the street behind it.
- **Scrub and hedges at 2 m** hide what is close to them, not the whole field: they only block the first and last 60 m of a line of sight, and never around a vehicle.
- **MANPADS and anti-tank missiles obey the same map**: a Stinger needs its own clear line to the helicopter, and a Kornet or TOW gunner has to hold the reticle on the target for the whole flight.
- **An unidentified enemy stays a generic contact** until you get close enough to recognise it, or until it opens fire.

## 📦 Download and install

**Requirements:** Broken Arrow 1.2.0.3 on Steam, Windows. The DLCs are optional. MelonLoader 0.7.3 is included in the download ([github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader](https://github.com/LavaGang/MelonLoader/releases), guide at [melonwiki.xyz](https://melonwiki.xyz/)).

### Install

1. Click the green **DOWNLOAD** button at the top of this page. It opens the file on Google Drive: click **Download**, then extract the archive (Windows 11, 7-Zip or WinRAR). It holds the folder **BROKEN ARROW REALISM OVERHAUL**: the mod, MelonLoader, the music and the licence (`LICENSE.txt`). This page and its translations stay here on GitHub.
2. In Steam, right-click **Broken Arrow**, then **Manage**, then **Browse local files**.
3. Open the folder **BROKEN ARROW REALISM OVERHAUL** and copy `version.dll`, the `MelonLoader` folder, the `Mods` folder and the `UserData` folder into the game folder, next to `BrokenArrow.exe`. The `UserData` folder holds the mod's music; without it the game keeps its own. `LICENSE.txt` is the licence and does not need to be copied.
4. Start the game from Steam with **Anti-Cheat Disabled**. The first launch takes a little longer while MelonLoader gets ready, and a console window opens: that is normal.
5. A **Mod** tab appears in the Options window. In the Arsenal, create a deck: choose NATO or CSTO, then your country.

<a id="update"></a>

### Update from an older version
Download the new archive with the **DOWNLOAD** button at the top of this page, copy the new files over the old ones, then open your decks and check them.

### Uninstall
Delete `Mods\BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll` and the `UserData\RealismOverhaul...` folders. To remove MelonLoader too, delete `version.dll` and the `MelonLoader` folder. The game's own divisions come back, exactly as before.

> [!TIP]
> Before uninstalling, keep a copy of your `Decks` folder (`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\SteelBalalaikaStudio\BrokenArrow\Decks`). Decks built on the mod's divisions only make sense with the mod.

> [!NOTE]
> **About the virus warning: it is a false positive.** MelonLoader places a native file, `version.dll`, next to the game under the name of a Windows system library, so the game loads it instead of the real one. That is how every MelonLoader mod for every Unity game works, and it is also, word for word, the technique antivirus engines are built to detect. The file is flagged for what it resembles, not for what it does. The mod itself, `BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll`, is one managed .NET file, with no native code, no packer, no obfuscation and no network access.

Not compatible with other mods that rewrite unit statistics.

<a id="not-possible"></a>

## 🚫 What is not possible, and I'd rather say it

- **A smoke trail hanging in the sky** for a minute after the hit. The trail belongs to the projectile: the game attaches it to the missile and takes it back the moment the missile dies. The mod holds it for the whole flight to impact, but a trail that hangs over the battlefield afterwards would mean shipping the game's own compiled effect files, and a mod should not do that.
- **A commander's aircraft only where the mission allows it.** In the campaign he uses an aircraft only on a map where the mission itself has flown one, because that is the only proven way to bring a plane in and back.
- **Bombs without wings still fall ballistically** from flight altitude, about 1.5 to 2.5 km ahead of the target. Glide kits are real stand-off weapons since 4.9.5, but they are not released at low altitude, and the skirmish commander does not give the precision-strike order.
- **No multiplayer in this release.** The game's lobbies and battles run on its official servers, and they can ban a modified client. The mod plays solo and offline.
- **Two sides only.** The game knows the American side and the Russian side, so NATO countries always fight CSTO countries.
- **Countries whose main equipment is not in the game**, such as France or the United Kingdom, cannot be made. When a single vehicle has no 3D model, a vehicle of the same class stands in, with the real name and the real stats.
- **Loitering munitions** wait for the game's own version. The Lancet and Switchblade team cards stay hidden until then.
