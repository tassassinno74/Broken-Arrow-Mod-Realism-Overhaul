Broken Arrow gives you real vehicles, real weapons and real doctrine, and then lets a 152 mm howitzer drop a shell on the tank parked next to it, lets a machine gun swat an aircraft three kilometres up, and caps every missile at the 9 km edge of its old maps.

This mod rewrites **more than 12,000 values** with real-world data and rebuilds the game around them: real armour, engines, rates of fire, loadouts and published ranges up to 100 km, every price set on real combat value, and an **enemy commander** who only knows what his own units see. You fight as a **nation**, one of thirteen countries in two camps, NATO and CSTO, with the equipment it really fields, and you **choose your realism**: Vanilla, Realistic or Semi-realistic.

You play the campaign, the official scenarios, the skirmish and the mod's own modes against the AI (**Logistics Front**, **Breakthrough**, **Command Post Hunt**) alone, or together in the mod's own lobbies: co-op against the AI with up to 5 players, or PvP between mod players up to 5 v 5. The official servers are never used.

**6.1** is built for game **1.2.2**: the crash while a map loads is fixed. It adds a **battle log** you control (Off, On or Full, nothing sent over the Internet), lighter memory use and a **Low-end PC mode**, a **new HUD** with movable blocks and a size setting, rebuilt **lobbies** and **Create a game**, a **Guide** and a **Training centre** in the Play tab, a scenario AI commander that follows the difficulty, real Chinese missiles and new loadouts, and many fixes. [What's new in 6.1.](#new-61)

**6.0.3** gives China **its own vehicles**, with their real data and real weapons that turn and fire, lets **every vehicle tow** another one by its real weight and gives **a clean icon to every button** of the mod. It also lets the host choose the **number of players** (up to 5 in co-op, up to 5 v 5 in PvP), sets the **unit cap per player**, sends **one enemy commander for every two players**, opens the **official missions** to co-op, adds host settings for ranges, aircraft spawn and abandonment, and brings a C-RAM that fights shells and rockets, real lines of sight over forests and new real loadouts. [What's new in 6.0.3.](#new-603) **6.0.2** makes **China playable**, with new infantry, makes tanks really fire on buildings, gives Breakthrough a real 3-to-1 attack and the Logistics Front AI commander a player's economy. [What's new in 6.0.2.](#new-602) **6.0.1** is built for the final game 1.2.1: it fixes saved decks, abandoned vehicles, buildings hit by cruise missiles and the launch with anti-cheat on, and adds an Automatic / Manual laser choice. [What's new in 6.0.1.](#new-601) It builds on **6.0**, made for the game's 1.2.1 update (Unity 6). 6.0 adds a Vanilla realism mode and a Dynamic unit cap, keeps vehicle headlights off in battle, makes helicopters pay for flying low, gives the AI a sharper picture of the battlefield, makes artillery leave battery before it moves, and opens the door to sub-mods with the BARO Workshop. ## 🆕 What's new in 6.1

> **The 6.1 in five lines**
> - **Built for game 1.2.2**: the crash while a map loads is fixed.
> - **A battle log** you control: Off, On or Full. Nothing is sent over the Internet.
> - **A new HUD**: one style, blocks you can move, a size you can set. The old one stays available.
> - **Rebuilt lobbies** and Create a game, a **Guide** and a **Training centre** in the Play tab.
> - **Real weapons**: Chinese missiles, new bombs, Tu-95, F-15EX, B-1B, Eurofighter.

> From 6.0.3, close the game, replace `Mods\BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll` and copy the new `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` folder into the game folder (let Windows replace the files). **Every player in a lobby needs 6.1.** The first launch after the game update takes a few more minutes while MelonLoader rebuilds its files.

### 🔄 Game 1.2.2
- With 6.0.3 on game 1.2.2, the game could crash or get stuck while a map loads. This is fixed.
- The whole mod has been checked again on the new game version.

### 📝 Battle log (diagnostics)
- New setting in **Esc > Options > Mod > Diagnostics**: **Off**, **On** or **Full**.
- It writes a log of each battle to `UserData\RealismOverhaul_Sonde` (the last 10 are kept). **On**: errors, slowdowns, memory and the battle summary (about 200 KB). **Full**: also the details of the mod's scripts and of the AI (about 3 MB).
- It records the Steam ID (or VK Play account) and nickname of every player in the battle, and the chat. Never an IP address; your Windows folder (with your user name) is hidden.
- **Nothing is sent over the Internet.** **Prepare the file to send** puts the last 5 battles into one `.zip` under 10 MB: you decide whether to send it, and you send it yourself.

### 💾 Memory and low-end PCs
- Lighter memory use: a 3D model is only read when a unit of that type appears on the map. Before, the memory could fill up at the start of a battle.
- **Low-end PC mode** (Esc > Options > Mod): **Auto** (the mod decides from your PC's memory), **Yes** or **No**.

### 🖥️ A new HUD
- **Mod's new HUD** (Esc > Options > Mod > Interface (HUD)): one style for all of the mod's battle display (the same colours, one background, the game's font), one badge per unit.
- **Layout mode**: drag each block of the mod with the mouse, then **Done**. Positions are kept on your PC, one set per screen shape.
- **Mod interface size**: 75 % to 150 %.
- **Mod's new HUD: No** brings back the old display, unchanged. These settings are for your PC only: nothing is sent to other players.

### 🌐 Lobbies and Create a game
- **Find a lobby** rebuilt: your version and the game's, the host's rules before you join, filters (same version, friends...), and a clear **Wrong version** button for a lobby of another version.
- A new lobby screen: your side in blue, the other side in red.
- **Create a game** laid out in 7 sections. What is chosen when the lobby is created (game mode, side) stays locked while the lobby is open.

### 📖 Guide and Training centre
- Two new cards in the **Play** tab: **Guide** (the whole mod explained: game modes, realism, ranges, wounded, engineers, supply, artillery, air war, the battle log...) and **Training centre** (learn the mod step by step).
- Both are also on the mod's home page.

### 🧠 Scenarios: the AI commander follows the difficulty
- **Easy**: the scenario's script alone. **Normal**: the mod's enemy commander plays like a normal player. **Hard**: it plays much harder.
- **Create a game > Mod AI commander (scenario) > Off** turns it off at every difficulty. Scenario makers can also write `script_only = yes` in `baro_rules.txt`.

### 🚀 Real weapons and loadouts
- **Chinese missiles** on the Chinese aircraft and helicopters, which used to fire Russian or American missiles: PL-15, PL-10, TY-90, AKD-10, HJ-10, YJ-91, YJ-83K, KD-88, YJ-12 and KD-20.
- **New bombs**: GBU-38 and GBU-32 JDAM, GBU-16 Paveway II, FAB-5000M-54, OFAB-100-120.
- **Tu-95**: one card again. Tu-95M with its FAB-5000 or FAB-9000 bombs, Tu-95MS with its Kh-55.
- **F-15EX**: up to 12 air-to-air missiles. **B-1B**: 24 GBU-31 in its bay. **Eurofighter**: IRIS-T and Meteor loadouts.

### 🔧 Fixes
- **Chat**: one key press no longer types two letters, and messages arrive in the right order for every player.
- Smoke from wrecks rises straight.
- **WZ551**: its 25 mm gun is back. **Su-30MKK**: it fires its Kh-29 instead of circling the target.
- **Automatic laser**: it lights the target of the attack order.
- A tank sent to a lake shore no longer drives into deep water.

### 🚙 New vehicles
They arrive step by step, each one after its check in game.

## 🆕 What's new in 6.0.3

> **The 6.0.3 in five lines**
> - China gets **its own vehicles**, with their real data and real weapons that turn and fire.
> - **Every vehicle can tow** another one, by its real weight.
> - **A clean icon** for every button of the mod.
> - **Up to 5 players per side**, a unit cap per player and official missions in co-op.

> From 6.0.2, close the game, replace `Mods\BrokenArrowRealismOverhaul.dll` and copy the new `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` folder into the game folder (let Windows replace the files). **Every player in a lobby needs 6.0.3.**

### 🛡️ China: its own vehicles
Each Chinese vehicle now has its own 3D model, its real data and the real weapons of the game on it: the machine guns and remote weapon stations on the roofs are visible, turn and really fire. The deck only keeps what China really fields: the Tor-M1 card gives way to the HQ-17.

### ⛓️ Towing with every vehicle
- Any vehicle can now tow another one on a chain or a steel cable, as far as its weight allows: a tank tows anything, some infantry fighting vehicles can tow a tank, a car tows a car but not a tank.
- **Tow** (the hook): click it, then click the vehicle to tow. **Unload** (the crossed-out hook): click it, then click where to leave the vehicle.
- Bring back an abandoned or immobilised vehicle to the rear, where recovery vehicles repair it and its crew climbs back in.
- **Repair**: a recovery vehicle repairs fully. In a supply zone without one, repair is three times slower and stops at orange: the vehicle drives and fires again, but its damaged parts stay damaged. Engineers also repair up to orange. Repair costs supplies.

### 🔘 A clean icon for every button
- Every button of the mod has its own white icon, clear at a glance and in the style of the game: a hook to tow, a shield against missiles, a crosshair for fire at will, a radar for counter-battery, a helicopter on a rope for the fast rope, a mine for each kind of mine, and more.
- The artillery modes read clearly: **Fire at will** (every enemy your side has spotted) and **Counter-battery**.

### 🌐 Playing together
- **Number of players**, in **Create a game** and in the lobby screen: 1 to 5 against the AI (the game has 5 seats per side); 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 between players on the front maps, from 1 v 1 up to 5 v 5. Chosen by the host, locked at launch.
- **Unit cap per player**: 25, 50, 75, 100, 125 or 150 (100 by default). A side's total is that number times its players, and the AI across has the same total (450 at most, for performance). The menu shows the total ("100 per player, 4 players: 400 per side").
- **Dynamic cap**: no limit for the players; the AI starts at 150 and rises to the number of units the players have on the map as soon as it is larger. It never comes down during the battle (450 at most).
- **One enemy commander for every two players** on the front against the AI (1-2 players: 1; 3-4: 2; 5: 3), attacking from different sides.
- **Official missions in co-op**: a **Missions** tab in **Create a game** (the Russian and American missions, the tutorial excepted), playable alone or with up to 5 players on the same side, with the mod's commanders on top of the script (one for every two players). The solo campaign does not change.
- **Your side**: NATO, CSTO or **Mixed** (any deck in the scenarios; the AI takes the faction across).
- **For scenario and sub-mod makers**: the enemy AI's deck can be chosen, and a `baro_rules.txt` file (or a `[BARO]` block in the description) sets the side, the allowed countries, the AI's country and deck, banned families or cards, the bots, the level and the scenario's music (`music = ...`, `music_mode = replace | mix`), imposed on every player.

### ⚙️ New host settings (chosen before the game, the same for everyone)
- **Air defence range**: Realistic / 25 km / 15 km / Vanilla. **Missile and artillery range**: Realistic / 50 % / 25 % / Vanilla. In Vanilla mode, these lines are greyed out.
- **Aircraft spawn**: 1, 5 or 10 km behind the map edge; **helicopters**: 1, 2, 3 or 5 km.
- **Abandonment allowed**: Yes / No (No: vehicles fight to the death; aircraft crews keep their real chances of survival).

### 🎯 Combat
- **C-RAM** also targets mortar bombs, artillery shells, rockets and Grad salvos, with its real limits: a 2 km bubble, one target at a time, saturated by a big salvo, short bursts and a real chance per projectile.
- **Lines of sight**: never through a forest (treetops at 20 m); from high ground you see over it; a garrison looks out from the side of the building facing the target and from the top floor.
- **Buildings**: neighbouring sections no longer fall from a hit that does not touch them; each charge has its real radius.
- **Capture by balance of forces** on skirmish zones: a lone shooter no longer blocks a capture; the stronger side takes the zone, all the faster as it is stronger. The **Destruction** mode is back on the front: one large central zone.
- **Ships**: a launch point for each side on the maps with real sea on both sides.
- **T-80BVM**: its anti-drone roof now covers the whole turret, as on the real tanks.
- **Aviation**: no more aircraft shot down as it appears; no automatic climb toward an air target beyond 10 km (you climb with the button); big aircraft no longer crash in low flight; heavy guided bombs no longer fall straight down.
- **Real loadouts added**: UMPK glide bombs (FAB-500 and FAB-1500) on the Russian aircraft that carry them; SDB and StormBreaker (up to 28 SDB); HARM, JSOW and GBU-54; Ataka + Verba on helicopters; the StormBreaker hits a moving target. Grad anti-tank mine rockets can be fired as a full salvo.
- **Automatic laser**: it no longer picks abandoned vehicles.
- **Free camera**: up to 25,000 m.

### 🧠 AI
- **The AI ally is more active**: in Breakthrough it pushes into contested zones and sends reinforcements; on the front it buys like the enemy (infantry, air defence, reserve, operations) and holds a defensive line.
- **Both commanders call for fire support** and hunt the air defence they have seen.

### 🧩 Decks and cards
- **Sappers** are in the **Infantry** tab, with their transport (a notice says so at start-up: take them from there).
- **Official names** on the cards.
- **VDV Kornet squads**: 8 men, as in real life.

### 🔧 Fixes (player reports)
- The JSOW of the heavy bombers with its real rules; the LShO-57 loadout; Fallschirmjäger that did not move; "DLC2" names; mod warnings on two lines; empty pylons on some helicopters; pink glass in the Arsenal.
- **Highway of Death**: memory is tidied up during loading (graphs, and trees in a single pass).
- **Wounded infantry** is healed again at your supply points, and healing no longer drains the supply crate.
- **Missiles fired through a gun** (Invar, Sprinter, Arkan, Kombat, Shillelagh, Sokol-V) keep the range of the gun's shells when the host shortens missile ranges.
- **S-8 and S-13 rockets** of the helicopters fire again from 500 m (they were held back below 1,300 m and 1,100 m).

## 🆕 What's new in 6.0

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Replaces 5.9.5 and every older version. The game must be on version 1.2.1.** Delete your old `RealismOverhaul_Vehicules` folder before you copy the new ones: see [Install](#install). Your decks are kept: if a card changed, only that card leaves your saved deck, with a message. **6.0 is the minimum version to play together**: every player in a lobby needs 6.0.

### 🔄 Built for game 1.2.1
- **Compatible with Broken Arrow 1.2.1 (Unity 6)**: the game starts safely, the new rockets and missiles of 1.2.1 come with their real values, and the Arsenal choices broken by the update are back in place (helicopter loadouts, engineer teams).
- **A new Vanilla realism mode**: every card of the mod takes the stats of its base-game card. **Semi-realistic** now gives **+60 % health** to everything, aircraft included, and leaves the ranges alone.
- **Vehicle headlights stay off in battle.** The light of gunfire and explosions stays.

### 🗺️ Enlarged maps
- You can place your units anywhere on the map again (repaired for 1.2.1).
- The roads stretch with the map, smooth and with no zigzag.

### 🚁 Air war
- **Against helicopters in flight**: the 12.7 mm machine gun reaches up to 1,000 m; 5.56 and 7.62 mm (rifles, machine guns, miniguns) barely hurt armored helicopters, while 12.7 mm and up keep their effect; a tank's coaxial machine gun does not fire higher than its gun can elevate (the commander's 12.7 mm and the small turrets still fire); a fast-moving helicopter is harder to hit for machine guns and cannons without radar (radar-guided guns unchanged); flying low costs more against cannons and machine guns; shoulder-fired missiles only fire when they can see their target; the radar missiles of SAM systems are blind against low flyers.
- **A lost aircraft or helicopter always takes longer to come back** than one that returns empty or intact: no more sacrificing a plane to get it back sooner.

### 🎯 Ranges, speeds and unit cap
- **Ranges and speeds checked against real figures**: practical range (where the weapon is accurate) and maximum range, missiles at their real engagement range, service speeds verified vehicle by vehicle.
- **A "Dynamic" unit cap**: no limit for the players, while the AI stops at 150 per side and follows you beyond that. The allied bots of the scenarios work again, and in solo Logistics Front you can have an AI ally.

<a id="ground-war"></a>

## 🪖 Ground war

### Weapons like real life
- **Every weapon** has its real rate of fire, magazine, reload and bursts, real calibre, penetration and dispersion; one projectile on screen is one real round. Weapons are very accurate at point-blank range, and anti-aircraft guns have their real spread.
- The unit card shows a **Ranges (direct fire)** line: practical range, maximum range, and how the weapon aims (iron sights, scope, thermal). Gunners aiming by eye are limited by what the eye can do.
- **Damage depends on calibre** against real armour: a round that does not penetrate in real life does no damage, and the cards show the real millimetres. An APFSDS round loses about 4 % of its penetration per km.
- Shaped-charge anti-tank missiles do not fire at infantry, and anti-tank teams keep their missiles for armour.

### Damaged parts
When a hit goes through a ground vehicle, it can strike a part, chosen by the face hit: **tracks** (immobilised), **wheels or engine** (slowed, then immobilised), **engine fire**, **turret** (slower aim), **gun** (inaccurate), **optics** (sees less far), **crew** (slower), or the **ammunition**, which can cook off, with a real chance for each tank. A hit that does not go through can still damage the optics, the sights, the turret or the tracks, or stun the crew. An immobilised vehicle only turns its turret. Supply trucks, repair trucks and engineers repair.

### Suppression
Stress rises with hits **and near misses**, up to **shaken** and then **panicked**: shaken infantry shoots wider, panicked infantry much wider, and still crawls forward. Vehicles are less affected. Calm returns about 40 seconds after the fire stops, and suppression is the same for every player in co-op.

### Infantry and buildings
- **Real team sizes** (2 to 4 men for a Javelin, TOW, Kornet, Stinger or Igla team, 8 for a VDV Kornet squad), smoke grenades for every combat squad, and a sprint of about 300 m.
- **Carried units die with their carrier**: after a heavy cannon, a missile or a bomb, nobody gets out; survivors of lighter fire come out wounded.
- **Buildings by the host's realism mode**: Realistic like real life (a bomb blows away a wooden house, only the part that is hit collapses), Semi-realistic 2 to 3 times sturdier. Each charge has its real radius, and neighbouring sections no longer fall from a hit that does not touch them. Bullets, 20 to 40 mm guns and grenades do nothing to walls.
- **City fighting**: infantry in a building is hard to drive out with small arms, and a single ordinary shell no longer wipes out a squad inside; explosives, thermobarics, artillery and tank guns still work.
- Infantry bodies stay for the duration you chose, even off-screen.
- **Standing soldiers are treated** at a depot, a supply truck or the base; snipers reach their real practical range.

<a id="entrench"></a>

### Digging in and fortifications
- **Five real levels** for infantry and vehicles that stay in place, up to 45 minutes, faster near supply, with engineers or with a dozer blade. Each level makes the unit harder to spot, to hit and to drive out.
- **Two kinds of engineers** in every country: **sappers** lay and clear mines (**Clear a mine lane**) and repair vehicles; **construction engineers** fortify buildings and carry a depot's stock inside. Engineers can fortify a whole area with visible walls.
- **Special forces and recon** move, camouflage and aim faster.

### Mines
- **Engineers** lay anti-tank mines in a line; a mine is live the moment it lands, and **any vehicle** that rolls over it is destroyed, friend or foe.
- **Remote mining**, the same number on each side: RAAM shells for the United States, 9M28K rockets for Russia, 10 km range, mines that stay for the whole game, at most 100 per side. Grad anti-tank mine rockets can also be fired as a full salvo.
- **Mine clearing**: mine-clearing vehicles, sappers, and KMT ploughs with their **Mine-clearing mode**.
- Mines are shared by every player in co-op, with their explosion and crater.

### Artillery
- **Real loadouts and stowage**, guided shells (Krasnopol-M, Excalibur, Vulcano...), real **aiming times** and **minimum ranges**.
- **In battery**: after every move, a gun goes through a real emplacement time before it fires, and it leaves battery before it drives off (30 s self-propelled, 120 s towed). The AI calls for its fire (60 s) and adjusts it.
- **Two modes**, together if you wish: **Fire at will** (every enemy your side has spotted) and **Counter-battery**, on a battery located by sound.
- **Remote mining** shells and rockets, and **cluster munitions** with their real effectiveness.

### Movement and fire orders
- **Right-click** = by road; **double right-click** = across country; **B** = back to base by road. Empty unarmed trucks drive home on their own.
- **Attack** (**Q** on QWERTY, **A** on AZERTY) is the game's attack-move.
- **G** is **Suppressive fire** for ground vehicles: the vehicle moves up within range, then fires a long burst (200 autocannon rounds, 5 tank shells, 50 grenades, 100 machine gun rounds), on a unit or on a point on the ground. Neither G nor Attack fires through two separate stretches of forest.
- Autocannon and heavy machine gun bursts **fell trees**, and the view opens for both sides.

### Explosions and sound
- **Real blast and fragmentation** for every explosive, and trees flattened within the real blast radius. A destroyed vehicle explodes with the explosive it still carries.
- **Sound travels as far as it really does**: a ballistic missile 15 km, a howitzer 10 km, a tank gun 5 km, a rifle 2 km, with echoes at the speed of sound.
- Real light at launches and explosions, smoke and dust when a tank fires, trails of about 30 seconds that spread out.

<a id="air-war"></a>

## ✈️ Air war

### Real self-protection, real seekers
Every aircraft and helicopter carries its **real self-protection suite** (flares, chaff, jammer, DIRCM, towed decoy, or nothing at all) and pays for it. Decoys go out on their own at the missile warning. What fools a missile depends on **its seeker**, for 265 guided munitions:

| Seeker | Chance to be fooled |
|---|---|
| Old infrared | 70 % by flares |
| Second-generation infrared | 30 to 55 % by flares |
| Imaging infrared | 15 % by flares |
| Infrared against a DIRCM | 50 to 90 % |
| Old radar | 35 % by chaff |
| Modern radar | 10 % by chaff |
| Anti-radiation, GPS, ballistic | Never |

In Semi-realistic, each countermeasure succeeds 25 % more often. Several add up, never beyond 95 %.

### Aircraft and helicopters
- **Real manoeuvrability**: every fighter turns at its real rate, those with thrust vectoring first, and can **dodge a missile**. Your fighters with no order hunt the enemy aircraft your side sees, then come home when hit or short of ammunition. They never climb on their own toward an air target beyond 10 km: you climb with the button.
- **Stealth against radar only**: stealth aircraft are found by radar much closer, by the age of the radar, while eyes and infrared see them like any other aircraft.
- **Real engines**: real thrust, climb and turn.
- **Real endurance** on every map and a **real turnaround time** at base (5 to 45 minutes). Helicopters can **land**, engine off.
- **Aircraft appear 1, 5 or 10 km and helicopters 1, 2, 3 or 5 km outside the map, behind their own side**, as the host chooses. No aircraft is shot down as it appears.
- **Low flight** hides from long-range radar missiles but hands you to every gun in reach, and big aircraft no longer crash in it; **high flight** is seen by all, up to the real ceiling of the air defence.

### Strike
- **Glide bombs** at their real stand-off (50 to 100 km), released with the precision-strike order; guided bombs of several calibres are all released.
- **Toss strike** for the aircraft that really do it; **Rocket salvo** button (25, 50, 75 or 100 % of the unguided rockets per pass).
- **Over 160 real loadouts** on the right pylons, among them UMPK glide bombs (FAB-500 and FAB-1500), SDB and StormBreaker (up to 28 SDB), HARM, JSOW, GBU-54, and Ataka + Verba on helicopters; loadouts the real aircraft never carried are hidden. The StormBreaker hits a moving target, and heavy guided bombs no longer fall straight down.
- **Real missile profiles**: the Kinzhal, the Zircon and the ARRW fly the way they really do; Javelin, Spike and Hellfire dive onto the roof.
- **Laser designation** on the units that really carry a designator: **Automatic** or **Manual** (Esc > Options > Mod); a laser you set yourself is never moved, and the automatic laser never picks abandoned vehicles.
- **Kamikaze drones**: five engines, three warheads, and the **DIVE HERE** button. Jet kamikaze drones can only be shot down by missiles.

### Air defence
- **Two anti-air missile hits bring down anything that flies.** A 12.7 mm machine gun reaches a helicopter in flight up to 1,000 m; cannons without radar reach helicopters at 1,500 m at most.
- **You set each launcher**: missiles, helicopters, aircraft, drones. **Anti-missile** and **Rocket/bomb intercept** switches on the systems built for it.
- **C-RAM** also targets mortar bombs, artillery shells, rockets and Grad salvos, with its real limits: a 2 km bubble, one target at a time, saturated by a big salvo, short bursts and a real chance per projectile.
- **The host sets the ranges**: air defence Realistic, 25 km, 15 km or Vanilla; missiles and artillery Realistic, 50 %, 25 % or Vanilla.
- **A radar that is on gets picked up** by enemy aircraft; switch it off to hide.

## 🧠 The enemy commander

A second commander plays the enemy side of every campaign mission next to the mission script, takes the place of the computer players in scenarios and skirmishes, and is the only enemy in the modes vs AI.

- **A player's eyes**: he sees exactly what the game shows his side, nothing more, and decides after a staff delay. **No hidden advantage.**
- **One commander for every two players** on the front against the AI: 1 for 1-2 players, 2 for 3-4, 3 for 5, attacking from different sides.
- **You choose his country**, one or several of the other camp, and he bears the name of its head of state (for the United States, General Dan Caine).
- **He reads what you field and answers it**: your helicopters bring surface-to-air missiles, your artillery brings counter-battery, your armour brings anti-tank teams and a flanking group.
- **Defence first**: a real defensive line with its air defence and a reserve, recon before every attack, ambushes at bridges, crossroads and forest edges.
- **Disciplined fire**: guns that move after every salvo, air defence that never rides in the assault column, aircraft that never fly into known air defence. He calls for fire support and hunts the air defence he has seen.
- **He learns**: a technique that fails is not used twice on the same objective, and an axis where he lost units is avoided.
- **He spends like you**: a real wallet, cards like yours, supply trucks, engineers and sappers.
- **He never breaks a mission**: he checks the script before adding anything.

**An AI ally** can fight at your side when you play alone against the AI, with the same brain as the enemy. In Breakthrough it pushes into contested zones and sends reinforcements; on the front it buys like the enemy (infantry, air defence, reserve, operations) and holds a defensive line. It also calls for fire support and hunts the air defence it has seen.

## 👁️ Line of sight, weather and water

- **Sight never goes through a forest**: treetops stand at 20 m, and from high ground you see over them. A thick forest blocks direct fire and operator-guided missiles.
- **Heights are measured** on the game's own height map, and a flattened building stops hiding the street behind it. A garrison looks out from the side of the building facing the target and from the top floor.
- **An unidentified enemy stays a generic contact** until you get close or it opens fire.
- **Infantry that stops sees further** (binoculars), recon that stops even further (thermal), troops on the run see less. The same for the AI.
- **Weather**: rain and fog shorten sight a little for everyone, and the weather can change during the game. A **wind** drawn for each battle carries the smoke.
- **Water**: vehicles that cannot swim never drive into it and take the bridges; amphibious vehicles swim at their real speed, and infantry wades the fords. A vehicle in the water is hit as on land. On the maps with real sea on both sides, each side has a launch point for its ships.
- **Battle icons** stay visible when you zoom out, sized to the camera distance. The fog of war is unchanged.

## ❓ Questions players ask

- **Can I still play the official multiplayer?** Yes, once the mod and MelonLoader are removed (see [Uninstall](#update)). Never with MelonLoader in the game folder.
- **Do I need the DLCs?** No. Units from a DLC you do not own simply do not appear. Germany and the Baltic states need the Baltic DLC.
- **Are my decks kept after an update?** Yes. If a card changed, only that card is replaced or leaves your deck, with a message.
- **Can I play with friends who do not have the mod?** No: everyone in a lobby needs the mod, in the same version.
- **Is it too hard?** Choose Semi-realistic (the default) and lower the ranges of air defence, missiles and artillery in the host settings.
- **How do I report a bug?** Click the game's bug button: the mod's **Report a mod bug** window gathers the files to send.

## 🚫 What is not possible, and I'd rather say it

- **Two sides only.** The game knows the American side and the Russian side, so NATO countries always fight CSTO countries.
- **Countries whose main equipment is not in the game**, such as the United Kingdom, cannot be made on their own, and France is still in preparation.
- **No play on the official servers.** The mod plays solo and, together, only in its own lobbies.
- **Everything coming back after a crash.** A player who rejoins gets his units, their health and his money back, but felled trees, craters and wrecks made while he was away do not show, and there is no spectator mode.
- **Bombs without wings still fall ballistically**, and a commander's aircraft in the campaign only comes where the mission itself flies one.

## 🐞 Known issues

- The Highway of Death needs a lot of memory (30 GB recommended) and loads longer; an enlarged map takes a few more seconds to load.

## 💬 Feedback and credits

**Something went wrong?** Click the game's bug button 🐞, in the menu or in battle: the **Report a mod bug** window opens the right folders for you. The mod writes its log to `MelonLoader\Latest.log`, next to `BrokenArrow.exe`, and older logs to the `Logs` folder; the game writes its own to `GameLogs`. Send them **before you start the game again**, on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/brokenarrowrealism) or in an issue on this repository.
